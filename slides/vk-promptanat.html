The full exercise
Here is the prompt you paste in part one, drawn to scale by words. Look at where the words go. Only a handful say what to build. Far more say what it returns in the edge cases, what to do with bad input, and what must never happen: overflow must never wrap, a huge list is rejected before it is read, the list and its expiration never change. Then how to build it, how to make it reproducible, and the biggest block: how to prove it works, in a real, disposable Valkey. It ends with report honestly: do not claim success from a compile or a PING. Most of a good prompt is about failure, because that is where an agent guesses.

Codex best practices
Here is a nice insight straight from OpenAI's Codex best practices page. Model and reasoning effort are separate dials, and they recommend turning them in opposite directions. For the smallest model, Luna, start with high effort: give it room to think. For the most capable model, Astra, start light, which is low in the config file. That is their starting point, not a law, so test it on your own task. The same page gives a rough guide: low for fast, well-scoped tasks, medium or high for complex changes and debugging, extra high for long, agentic, reasoning-heavy work.

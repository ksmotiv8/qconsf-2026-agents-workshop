Take a minute. How long did it take you to write that post? Which step changed it the most? What did you end up fixing yourself? For me, AI doesn't make writing free. It makes it easier. It gets me past writer's block: once there is a draft on the screen, my instinct to correct it takes over quickly, and I'm editing instead of staring at a blank page. You bring the substance. The model can shape words, but only you know what you saw and why it matters. That is why the brief is the center of the workflow: get interviewed, approve the brief, and hold every draft to it. Skills shape the words, not the facts. humanizer and your voice change how it sounds; they cannot add what you did not give them. And edit it yourself. Use a fresh model to tell you what is unclear, then make the fixes with your own hands. The one that wrote it is the worst reviewer of its own work.

The full exercise
You have seen how humanizer is built. Now build your own skill, from two or three of your own posts in a samples folder. No samples? Skip this step. One: ask for up to three habits of your writing, each with a quote, and have it wait. Delete any habit that isn't you before you approve, because it is much harder to find once it is buried in a skill. The prompt also says phrasing only, never facts. Without that line, voice skills start inventing things to sound like you. You don't have to explain skills; the harness knows where they go. Two: start a new session so the skill loads, and check it with slash skills. Three: write from the same brief, with one more line: use my-voice. Does it sound more like you? Did it add anything that isn't in your brief? In our runs, it helped on some models and added new tells on others. The next step catches those.

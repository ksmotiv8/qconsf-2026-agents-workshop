You have all read text like this. The giveaway is rarely one word. It is the shape: the not-X-but-Y contrast, everything in threes, the question you ask so you can answer it, the same handful of words, a dash in every sentence, explaining what the reader just understood, and the chatbot pleasantries left in. Once you see these you cannot unsee them, and neither can the person you send it to. They stop reading and start discounting. So, how do you stop it? No walls of text: if the model gave you four paragraphs, your reader wanted one. Read before you send: you own every word that goes out under your name. Create signal, not noise: the model makes words cheap, which makes attention the scarce thing. Next you will see this for yourself.

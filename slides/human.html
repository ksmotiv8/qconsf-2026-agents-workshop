Pillar 1 · The human

You bring the outcome

Know what you want back
Research, or something that works.
Make the harness capture your intent
So it drives toward what you want.
Break big asks into steps
Smaller steps keep the agent focused.
Use Claude Code, Codex or mo, not the ChatGPT or Claude chat apps.
Start with the human, because everything starts with what you want. You are almost always after an outcome. Sometimes that is research, sometimes it is a working thing. Your job is to convey that intent so the harness captures it and drives toward it. When the ask is big, break it into steps so the agent stays focused. And do this in Claude Code, Codex, or mo, not the chat apps: the harness can touch your files, run things, and verify. Next, we try this with writing.

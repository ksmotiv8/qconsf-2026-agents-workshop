Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and model pages, November 2026.
Open-weight models are moving fast: Kimi, DeepSeek, GLM, and more every month. They are much cheaper per token, but cost per token is a head fake. In our own tests, open models were about a fifth of the price per token and about half the price per task, because they took more tokens and more turns. You can run them in the same harnesses: OpenCode out of the box, Codex with dash dash oss, or Claude Code pointed at a gateway. Own the weights, own the tradeoffs: you choose the host, the data policy, and the latency, and you also own the evals and the ops.

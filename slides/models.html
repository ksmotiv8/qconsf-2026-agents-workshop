Both tools give you a ladder of models. Claude Code: Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, Fable. Codex: Luna, Terra, Sol, Astra. Switch with slash model. Cheaper does not mean dumber. GPT-6.2-Sol scores the same on the Artificial Analysis index as last generation's flagship, at a fifth of the price. Sonnet 5.5 beats the Opus from July. But look at cost per task, not cost per token. Sonnet 5.5 and Sol charge the same per token, yet on the same benchmark Sonnet used about five times as many tokens, so the same task cost about five times as much. Cost per token is a head fake. And effort is a dial. The same Opus 5.5 scores 35 at low effort and 59 at max. For a LinkedIn post, low is plenty. For a gnarly refactor, turn it up. Prices and scores change monthly. Your own evals matter more than any model picker.

Before we start: your mileage may vary. Your post won't look like your neighbor's, and it won't look like mine. These three came from the same prompt today, on two models. One played it safe, one left blanks for me to fill in, one made up a conversation I never had. That's fine. The point of this exercise is to learn how to work with the model and how to steer it to the answer you want. The variability is a good thing to practice overcoming. Read what you get, and tell it what to fix.

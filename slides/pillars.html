The agentic loop
Three pieces: you, the harness, and the model. The harness is Claude Code, Codex, mo, or OpenCode: it holds the tools, the repo, and the tests. The model decides what to do next. The agentic loop runs through all three: the model asks for a tool, the harness runs it, the result goes back to the model, and it repeats until the work is done or it needs you.
